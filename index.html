Safety First, Always

  • Every piece we sell is a play / novelty item — there is no sharpened edge or functional blade on anything in our catalog.
  • Products are recommended for ages 13 and up and adult supervision for younger flip-trick learners.
  • These are not marketed or sold as weapons, tools, or combat-ready replicas. They're for practice, cosplay, fidgeting, and display.
  • Local laws on knife-shaped novelty items vary — please check your local regulations before ordering, especially for shipping to schools or restricted areas.
  • 3D-printed plastic can chip or break under extreme stress — don't use these as substitutes for real tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these actually sharp?

No. Every model is intentionally printed with rounded, blunt edges and tips. They look the part but can't cut.

What material are they printed in?

Most models use PLA+ for detail and rigidity; balisong trainers are offered in PETG on request for extra durability during flipping.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes! Mention it in your order notes and we'll follow up to talk through what's possible with your reference or idea.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within the country only while we scale up — reach out and ask, and we'll let you know if we can make an exception.

What if my piece arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll print and ship a free replacement.
